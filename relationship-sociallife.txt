Purpose: Measures overall satisfaction with a current romantic relationship.

Items: 7
Response scale: 1–5
Scoring range: 7–35
Higher score: Greater relationship satisfaction

Scoring

Items 4 and 7 are reverse-scored:

- 1 → 5
- 2 → 4
- 3 → 3
- 4 → 2
- 5 → 1

Then calculate:

Total RAS Score = Sum of all 7 scored items

Also display:

Mean Score = Total Score ÷ 7

Mean range: 1.00–5.00

Result interpretation for the app

Total Score| Display
7–17| Lower relationship satisfaction
18–24| Moderate relationship satisfaction
25–35| Higher relationship satisfaction

Important: These ranges should be treated as descriptive app bands, not diagnostic or universally validated clinical cut-offs. The original RAS establishes higher scores as indicating greater relationship satisfaction, but does not establish these three categories as clinical thresholds.

FRIENDS — Friendship Network Satisfaction

Purpose

To assess the user's satisfaction with their current friendship network, including closeness, emotional connection, communication, and social interaction.

Scale: Friendship Network Satisfaction (FNS) Scale
Items: 14
Response: 0–5
Scoring: Higher score = greater friendship-network satisfaction
Time frame: Friendships/contact during the past year

---

Instructions shown to the user

Please rate how much you agree with each statement about your friends, including your best friends, close friends, and other friends you have had contact with during the past year.

Response options

Score| Response
0| Not at all agree
1| Hardly agree
2| Somewhat agree
3| Pretty much agree
4| Very much agree
5| Completely agree

---

Questionnaire

Closeness

1. I feel close to my friends.

2. It is hard to imagine my life without my close/best friends.

3. My friends celebrate my good news.

4. I have fun with my friends.

5. I have meaningful conversations with my friends.

6. I like to hang out with my friends.

7. My friends understand me.

8. When I have a problem, I can talk to my friends about it.

Socializing

9. I spend a lot of time socializing with my friends.

10. My friends and I go out and do things together.

11. My friends and I eat together often.

12. I attend social events with my friends.

13. I spend free time with my friends.

14. I socialize with a lot of different people.

These are the published 14 items of the FNS Scale.

---

SCORING

Total Score

Add the scores for all 14 items.

Minimum = 0
Maximum = 70

Formula:

"FNS Total Score = Item 1 + Item 2 + ... + Item 14"

There are no reverse-scored items.

Mean Score

"Mean Score = Total Score ÷ 14"

Range: 0–5

Higher scores indicate greater satisfaction with the user's friendship network.

---

RESULT SCREEN

Example

Your Friendship Satisfaction

52 / 70

Mean: 3.71 / 5

«Your responses indicate a relatively high level of satisfaction with your friendships.»

What this means

Your responses reflect how close, supported and socially connected you currently feel with your friends.

The FNS measures friendship-network satisfaction; it does not diagnose loneliness, mental illness, or determine whether a friendship is objectively healthy or unhealthy.

---

IMPORTANT FOR INTERPRETATION

The original FNS research establishes that higher scores represent greater friendship-network satisfaction, but it does not establish clinical cut-offs such as “low,” “moderate,” and “high.” Therefore, the app should not present invented clinical thresholds as if they were validated.

For the prototype, if you want simple user-facing bands, label them explicitly as app-generated descriptive ranges, not clinical cut-offs.

For example:

0–23: Lower satisfaction
24–46: Moderate satisfaction
47–70: Higher satisfaction

Developer note: These are descriptive prototype bands based on the total score range and are not validated diagnostic thresholds.

Scientific note for developer

The FNS was developed as a 14-item measure with two content domains—closeness (8 items) and socializing (6 items)—but the researchers found sufficient evidence to support a general overall score. Therefore, for SUHRIT, use the total FNS score as the primary result, rather than presenting separate “Closeness” and “Socializing” scores as validated standalone results.
