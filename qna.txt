SUHRIT – WELL-BEING SECTION

Overall Structure

The Well-being section should have 3 parts:

1. Well-being Assessment

WHO-5 Well-Being Index
→ Validated psychological well-being assessment
→ Measures well-being over the last 2 weeks

2. Daily Check-in

→ Mood
→ Stress
→ Energy
→ Sleep duration
→ Physical activity

These are tracking features, not part of the WHO-5.

3. My Progress

→ WHO-5 score history
→ Sleep trends
→ Activity trends
→ Mood, stress and energy trends

---

1. WHO-5 WELL-BEING ASSESSMENT

Heading:

How have you been feeling lately?

Instruction:

«Please indicate which of the following statements best describes how you have felt over the last two weeks.»

Questions

Q1.

I have felt cheerful and in good spirits.

Q2.

I have felt calm and relaxed.

Q3.

I have felt active and vigorous.

Q4.

I woke up feeling fresh and rested.

Q5.

My daily life has been filled with things that interest me.

---

Response options

Every question should have the same six options:

Response| Score
All of the time| 5
Most of the time| 4
More than half of the time| 3
Less than half of the time| 2
Some of the time| 1
At no time| 0

The user selects one response per question.

---

2. WHO-5 SCORING

Step 1 – Raw score

Add the scores of all 5 questions.

Raw score = Q1 + Q2 + Q3 + Q4 + Q5

Possible range:

0–25

Step 2 – Percentage score

WHO-5 percentage score = Raw score × 4

Possible range:

0–100

Higher score = better psychological well-being.

---

3. WHO-5 RESULT SCREEN

After submitting, display:

Your Well-being Score

XX / 100

Then provide a simple interpretation.

Score interpretation

0–28

«Your current well-being score is relatively low. You may benefit from exploring the support and well-being resources available in SUHRIT.»

29–49

«Your current well-being score suggests that your well-being may need some attention. Consider exploring the support and activities available in SUHRIT.»

50–100

«Your current well-being score is in a range generally associated with better well-being. Continue supporting your well-being through healthy routines and activities.»

Important note below the result:

«This assessment is not a diagnosis. A lower score does not mean that you have a mental health disorder. If you are concerned about your well-being, consider speaking with a qualified professional.»

For scores below 50, the app may additionally display:

«Would you like to explore some well-being support?»

Buttons:

Explore Support
View Professional Support
Continue to Home

---

4. WHO-5 RESULT HISTORY

The app should save each completed WHO-5 assessment with:

- Date
- Raw score (0–25)
- Percentage score (0–100)

Show a simple graph:

My Well-being Over Time

Example:

Date| Score
1 Sept| 64
15 Sept| 72
29 Sept| 68

The user should be able to see whether their score is changing over time.

Important:

Do not compare a user's score with other users or create a ranking.

---

5. DAILY CHECK-IN

This is separate from WHO-5.

Heading:

How are you doing today?

These questions are for personal tracking, not psychological diagnosis.

---

A. MOOD

“How are you feeling today?”

Options:

- Very low
- Low
- Okay
- Good
- Very good

Score internally from 1–5 if needed for graphs.

---

B. STRESS

“How would you describe your stress today?”

Options:

- Very low
- Low
- Moderate
- High
- Very high

Score internally from 1–5.

Higher score = greater perceived stress.

---

C. ENERGY

“How is your energy today?”

Options:

- Very low
- Low
- Moderate
- High
- Very high

Score internally from 1–5.

Higher score = greater energy.

---

6. SLEEP TRACKING

Keep this very simple.

Question 1:

What time did you go to bed?

→ Time picker

Question 2:

What time did you wake up?

→ Time picker

The app automatically calculates:

Sleep duration = Wake-up time − Bedtime

Example:

10:30 PM → 6:30 AM

Sleep duration: 8 hours

If the sleep period crosses midnight, the app should calculate it correctly.
